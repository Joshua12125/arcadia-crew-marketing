About

A small crew that treats a website like a site to be surveyed, not just designed.

Arcadia Crew Marketing is a digital marketing and eCommerce studio. We measure before we build: every project starts with a real audit — on-page SEO, off-page signals, speed, and Google Ads compliance — and the build that follows is scoped to what that audit turns up.

What we actually do

Day to day, that means building Google Ads-compliant landing pages for regulated and research-niche advertisers, building and rebuilding Shopify and WooCommerce stores until product pages hold up on mobile, and running the SEO and speed audits that tell a client where to spend next.

We work as an embedded team for clients who need an operator, not just a vendor — across store builds, campaign pages, and the social scheduling and email automation that keep a launch moving after the site goes live.

How we work

Compliance is a first-class requirement, not an afterthought: noindex handling, neutral language, and claim review are built into every regulated-niche page from the first draft, not patched in after a rejection.

Builds go through real iteration. A product page or landing page is rebuilt against feedback until the layout, the copy, and the compliance posture all agree — not shipped once and left alone.
